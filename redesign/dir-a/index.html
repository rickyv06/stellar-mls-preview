Where do I even start if I've never bought a home?

Start with a quick conversation. We talk about your goals, your timeline and what monthly payment feels comfortable. Then you connect with a lender to get preapproved so you know your real budget before we start touring homes.

My credit isn't great. Should I still reach out?

Absolutely. A lot of my clients started there. We can build a plan to get you ready, whether that's a few small fixes now or a bit more time.

Is there down payment assistance in San Antonio?

Yes, there are programs available through local, state and lender programs. Each one has its own rules, so ask me and we'll check what you may qualify for.

Do I have to pay you to help me buy?

In most cases buyers don't pay me out of pocket, because my compensation is usually covered by the seller or builder. We'll put it in writing before we start.
