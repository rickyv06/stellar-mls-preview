FAQ

Questions, answered.

See all questions →
What credit score do I need to buy a home?

There isn't one magic number. Different loan types have different requirements, and lenders look at your whole picture, not just the score.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan type and your situation. Some loans need very little down. Ask me and I'll connect you with a lender who can give you real numbers.

Do I need my own agent to buy a new build?

I highly recommend it. I just need to be with you or registered on your first visit, so reach out before you tour.

How does a VA loan work?

A VA loan is a benefit earned through service. It can allow eligible buyers to purchase with little or no money down and no monthly mortgage insurance.
